# SchoolOS Backlog (Post-MVP Scope)

The following capabilities are deliberately excluded from MVP to maintain relentless focus on the core school operating system:

1. **CBT (Computer-Based Testing)**: Online timed exams, question banks, automated proctoring.
2. **Full LMS & Virtual Learning**: Video lecture hosting, discussion forums, lesson plan marketplace.
3. **Automated Payroll & Tax Accounting**: Staff salary disbursement, PAYE pension deductions, full double-entry accounting ERP.
4. **Transport & Fleet Management**: Bus routing, GPS vehicle tracking, fuel monitoring.
5. **Hostel & Boarding Management**: Room allocation, dormitory roll calls, mess billing.
6. **Library & Inventory Asset Suite**: Barcode book lending, science lab consumable tracking.
7. **Native Mobile Apps (iOS/Android)**: Replaced in MVP by mobile-first responsive web application.
8. **AI Student Tutor**: Direct personalized student learning bot.
9. **School Public Website Builder**: CMS for marketing landing pages.
10. **Multi-Country Expansion**: Currency/tax adaptations outside Nigeria.
