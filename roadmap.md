# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Discovery/Repository Audit
Establish project state, constraints and source-of-truth docs.

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Supabase, auth, app shell.

## Phase 2 — Tenancy & RBAC
Schools, memberships, roles, RLS, permissions.

## Phase 3 — People & Classes
Students, guardians, teachers, staff, classes, subjects, sessions, terms.

## Phase 4 — Attendance
Capture, history, summaries, basic alerts.

## Phase 5 — Academics
Assessments, scores, grades, results, report cards.

## Phase 6 — Finance
Fee structures, invoices, balances, receipts.

## Phase 7 — Payments
Paystack initialization, webhook verification, reconciliation.

## Phase 8 — Communication
Announcements, templates, notification jobs, provider adapters.

## Phase 9 — Dashboard
School health metrics and management views.

## Phase 10 — Intelligence
Deterministic signals, risk rules, school health.

## Phase 11 — Action Center
Prioritization, assignments, actions, resolution/outcomes.

## Phase 12 — AI Assistant
Tool-grounded read and assisted workflows.

## Phase 13 — Hardening
Security, privacy, performance, accessibility, error handling.

## Phase 14 — Full Validation
E2E, regression, production build, pilot readiness.

## Phase 15 — Pilot
10-school pilot instrumentation, feedback, bug fixes and iteration.
