# Academic Specification

## Academic hierarchy
School → Session → Term → Class → Subject → Assessment → Score → Grade → Result → Report Card.

## Grading
Grade scales are configurable per school/session. Calculations are deterministic and versioned.

## Attendance
Statuses: PRESENT, ABSENT, LATE, EXCUSED.

## Academic signals
- Low attendance
- Significant performance decline
- Failed/low-performing subject
- Missing teacher submissions
- Class-level decline

## Report cards
Generate from authoritative result records. Teacher comments may be AI-drafted but require review before publication.
