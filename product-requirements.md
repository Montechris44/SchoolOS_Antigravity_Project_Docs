## Requirement priorities
P0 = required for MVP.
P1 = valuable immediately after MVP.
P2 = later expansion.

### P0
Auth, school tenancy, RBAC, students, parents, teachers, classes, sessions/terms, attendance, assessments, results, fees, invoices, payments, receipts, announcements, dashboard, deterministic signals, Action Center, read-only/assisted AI.

### P1
Advanced analytics, automated reminders, AI report comments, parent WhatsApp assistant, multi-campus, admissions, timetable.

### P2
CBT, LMS, AI tutor, transport, inventory, payroll, mobile apps, marketplace.
